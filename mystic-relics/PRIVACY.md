**Mystic Relics** ("the app", "we") is developed by {STUDIO}. This policy explains
what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device
Your game progress (level, coins, gems, settings, achievements) is saved **locally
on your device** using the browser/WebView local storage. We do not upload or sync
this data to any server.

## Advertising
The app shows ads via **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect
and process a device advertising identifier and related technical information
(approximate location from IP, device type, ad interaction). This is handled by
Google, not by us. See Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

## In-app purchases
Purchases (remove ads / currency packs) are processed by **Google Play Billing**.
We do not receive or store your payment details.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
data from children.

## Your choices
- Reset advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads.
- Remove ads: purchase the "Premium / no ads" item in the shop.
- Clear local data: clear the app's storage in device settings.
