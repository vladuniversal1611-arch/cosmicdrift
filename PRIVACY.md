## What the app collects

Hill Rush does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. The app has
no network permission and works fully offline.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: coins, upgrades, unlocked
vehicles and maps, records, missions, achievements and settings. This data is
never sent to us.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this data in
your device backup, under Google's backup terms.

## Permissions

- **Vibration** — short vibrations on crashes and stunts. You can turn this
  off in the game's settings.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.
