Privacy Policy — Bulbik Run

Developer: NEXIS · App ID: com.bulbik.nexis · Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Bulbik Run (“the Game”), published by NEXIS (“we”, “us”), handles and why. We designed the Game to work without any account and without collecting personal information ourselves.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The Game has no registration, no login, no chat and no in-app purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, levels, upgrades, skins, settings such as language and sound) is saved only on your device in the Game's local storage. It is never sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner in menus, occasional full-screen ads between runs, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic data related to ads.

This data is collected directly by Google and handled under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

4. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the Game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can choose to allow or refuse personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

5. Permissions

The Game uses only the Internet permission, which is needed to load ads. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data security and retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
